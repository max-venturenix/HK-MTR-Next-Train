## Tab covers and icons

The three tab covers were created with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-11-05.
Each is bundled as a transparent 1320 × 440 PNG and displayed proportionally across the full content width.
The following prompt summaries identify the scene in each file.

| Asset | Prompt summary |
| --- | --- |
| `favorites-cover.png` | Show Kiki and Lala collecting star tickets in an album at a cloud station, with a small train in the distance. |
| `search-cover.png` | Show the twins tracing their route on a folded train map beside a pastel train. |
| `facilities-cover.png` | Show the twins beside an open station lift, with a wheelchair sign and tactile paving. |

The tab icons are SVG drawings defined in `ui/component/LittleTwinStarsTabIcon.tsx`.
They pair Kiki with the Favorites star and Facilities wheelchair symbol, and Lala with the Search magnifier.
